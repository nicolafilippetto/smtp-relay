Authentication method

A certificate is more secure than a shared secret: only its public part is uploaded to Entra, while the private key stays encrypted here and never leaves the relay.

Client secret

When set, the system will email an alert to the configured admin each day starting from the configured warning window until you update the secret and the date.

→ Go to Notifications settings to configure the alert email address for the expiring credential.

Required only when you submit a new client secret — confirms the expiry date above reflects the new secret. Certificate expiry is tracked automatically.

Certificate credential

The relay generates the key pair itself — the private key is encrypted at rest and never downloadable. You upload only the public .cer to Entra. Because the app mints the certificate, its expiry date is known and tracked automatically.
